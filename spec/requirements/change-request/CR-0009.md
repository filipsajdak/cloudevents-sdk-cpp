# CR-0009: A Protobuf event format, hand-written, in two slices

> **Status, 2026-10-24.** `proposed`: the owner ruled four things (items 1 to 4 of "Owner decisions") and the rulings are recorded, but the mapping rules that the spike found lossy are open questions (the list under "Open questions for the owner"), and the status stays `proposed` until the owner answers them.
> The requirements are `reviewed` with no `satisfied_by` or `verified_by`, the status of CR-0004 stage 1 and CR-0005: `check_trace.py` requires a `verified_by` only from `approved`.
> Every requirement that an open question could change is worded to hold whichever way the question is ruled; the proposed wording of a rule that needs a ruling is in the question, not in a requirement.
> The decision is [ADR-0015](../../adr/0015-a-hand-written-protobuf-format.md). No library code, CMake file or CI file changes in this request.

## What prompted this

CR-0006 ranked six candidates and proposed a bet on the Protobuf format, first slice a format-only API, with a spike of at most one week, a pivot signal and a decision date of 2026-11-11.
The spike ran on 2026-10-22 (pull request 113, `docs/spikes/protobuf.md`, with goldens under `interop/protobuf-spike/`; merged).
On 2026-10-24 the owner read it and ruled that the bet goes ahead (items 1 to 4).
This request turns the spike into a specification: requirements, a decision, the cases that do not map and what to ask the owner.

The spike's result in one line: the Go and Java SDKs agree on the schema and on every attribute type, decode each other's bytes, and agree on bytes for 1 of 13 events; no case needs a break; a hand-written reader and writer measured 455 formatted lines and round-trips both SDKs.

## Owner decisions

On 2026-10-24:

1. **Protobuf is a go as a bet, and ships as an addition in `ce::v4`**: a new format header and target, and no change to a published declaration. Recorded with the pivot signal below.
2. **The codec is hand-written.** No `libprotobuf`, no generated code, and the core stays CTRE-only. The spike's prototype measured 455 lines formatted and round-trips both SDKs. The wording change to the dependency constraint that CR-0006 proposed for a format with a third-party library is therefore **not needed**, and no file named in that table is edited. Because the codec parses untrusted bytes, a fuzz target is required from the first slice (`SWR-PROTO-0017`); the limits on nesting, length and overflow are proposed below as named constants, for the owner to confirm (open question 1).
3. **Writing follows the specification, reading accepts everything.** The encoder writes `text_data` for string payloads; the decoder accepts `binary_data`, `text_data` and `proto_data` from either SDK. The Go SDK's difference is a known limitation and an interoperability finding (next section), recorded as D-INTEROP-5 recorded the Kafka findings.
4. **The field numbers follow `cloudevents.proto` at `v1.0.2`, not the prose of `protobuf-format.md`.** Evidence is in "The pivot signal and the evidence for the decisions".

## The pivot signal and the evidence for the decisions

**The pivot signal was met read literally, and the owner went ahead.**
The CR-0006 bet said: pivot to MQTT or stop if "the two SDKs disagree on a v1.0.2 MUST".
The spike found one: the specification says text data MUST be stored in `text_data`, and sdk-go has no code path that writes `text_data`; it writes `binary_data` for everything but `application/protobuf`.
The spike read this as wire-compatible and one SDK deviating, the shape D-NATS-1 already accepted, and left the signal to the owner.
**The owner ruled on 2026-10-24 to go ahead.** That is the record of the decision against the signal; the decision date of 2026-11-11 stays (open question 12).

| finding | evidence (from the spike) | consequence here |
|---|---|---|
| the prose and the `.proto` number differently | `.proto`: `attributes` 5, `binary_data` 6, `text_data` 7, `proto_data` 8. Prose: attributes 1, data 2 to 4. Both SDKs implement the `.proto`; the goldens decode against it with `protoc` | the `.proto` wins (decision 4, `SWR-PROTO-0003`); under rule 5 of `CLAUDE.md` the discrepancy is reported, and an upstream report is open question 13 |
| sdk-go never writes `text_data` | 13 goldens: `binary_data` for `text_data`, `text_data_empty`, `data_array` and `full` | the encoder follows the specification; the decoder accepts both |
| sdk-go quotes a JSON `text_data` | sdk-go reads a `text_data` of `application/json` and returns it as a JSON string (`"{\"count\":3,...}"`) | a known limitation of sdk-go as a reader; the interop test records it and does not hide it |
| the SDKs agree on bytes for 1 of 13 events | map order (random in sdk-go), empty `binary_data` for no data in sdk-go, `ce_uri` against `ce_uri_ref` for a relative `dataschema`, the `Any` type URL | no byte comparison; decode-equal comparison, and a deterministic encoder |
| neither SDK validates on decode | `spec_version` 0.3, an empty `id`, `time` sent as a string, a negative `nanos` all accepted | the decoder validates, as the JSON decoder does (`SWR-PROTO-0009`) |

## What is requested

**Slice 1: the format, bytes to event and event to bytes**, for a single event.

- `protobuf_format::encode(const event&) -> result<binary>` and `protobuf_format::decode(std::span<const std::byte>, protobuf::decode_options = {}) -> result<event>` (working names), header-only, standard library and `ce::core` only (`SWR-PROTO-0001`, `-0013`, `-0014`).
- The wire schema is `cloudevents.proto` at `v1.0.2`: required attributes as fields 1 to 4, every other attribute as a map entry, three data fields (`SWR-PROTO-0003`, `-0004`).
- Attribute types are kept: Protobuf carries them (`SWR-PROTO-0005`). A decoded `time` is UTC; the offset and the digits of the fraction are lost (`SWR-PROTO-0006`).
- The writer follows the specification, the reader accepts every form (`SWR-PROTO-0007`, `-0008`).
- Decode is strict and uses existing error codes (`SWR-PROTO-0009`), within named limits (`SWR-PROTO-0010`); encode is deterministic (`SWR-PROTO-0011`); an accepted input re-encodes (`SWR-PROTO-0012`).
- Evidence: offline goldens from sdk-go, sdk-java and `protoc` (`SWR-PROTO-0016`), a fuzz target (`SWR-PROTO-0017`), the guide (`SWR-PROTO-0018`), the module export in `ce::v4` only (`SWR-PROTO-0015`).

**Slice 2: structured mode in the bindings**, with the media type `application/cloudevents+protobuf`, as new entry points in the HTTP, Kafka and NATS bindings, and interoperability through them (`SWR-PROTO-0019` to `-0023`).
The batch format is in neither slice's requirements until the owner rules (open question 6).

### Error codes

Every decode failure maps to an enumerator that exists; none is added.

| failure | `errc` |
|---|---|
| truncation, a length beyond the buffer, a varint over ten bytes, an unskippable wire type (a group) | `parse_error` |
| an id, source or type absent or empty | `missing_required_attribute` |
| a `spec_version` other than `1.0` | `unsupported_spec_version` |
| a string that is not UTF-8 | `invalid_utf8` |
| a `ce_integer` outside the range of `int32`; input or map above a limit | `out_of_range` |
| an attribute value with no arm | `invalid_attribute_value` |
| `time`, `datacontenttype`, `dataschema` or `subject` in the wrong arm | `type_mismatch` |
| a Timestamp with nanos outside 0 to 999999999, or an instant the timestamp type cannot hold | `invalid_timestamp` |

## The mapping, and what does not map losslessly

Each row is from the spike's section 4 and the table of "Requirements this changes" follows from them. **Nothing in this table needs a change to a published declaration.**
A row marked **open** is an open question; its proposal is not a requirement until the owner rules.

| item | Protobuf form | what is lost or ambiguous | how it is specified (additions only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| boolean, integer, string, binary | scalar arms | nothing | `SWR-PROTO-0005` |
| URI, URI-reference | `ce_uri`, `ce_uri_ref` | the Java SDK chooses by absoluteness, the Go SDK writes `ce_uri` | write `dataschema` as `ce_uri` and an extension in its own arm; read either arm for `dataschema` and `source` (`SWR-PROTO-0005`) |
| timestamp | `Timestamp{seconds, nanos}` | the offset and the fraction digits (`SWR-CORE-0007`); years 1 to 9999 on the wire against about 1678 to 2262 in `ce::timestamp` | **open** (question 2); in the requirement: decode gives UTC, refuses what the type cannot hold with `invalid_timestamp` |
| `std::string` data | `text_data` | nothing | `SWR-PROTO-0007`, `-0008` |
| `binary` data | `binary_data` | nothing | same |
| JSON data (`json_text`, `json_document`) | `text_data` | read back as `std::string` or `binary`, not as JSON | **open** (question 4) |
| no data | no field (Java), empty `binary_data` (Go) | empty `binary_data` against no data | **open** (question 3) |
| `proto_data` | `Any{type_url, value}` | the `Any` type URL when it differs from `dataschema`; "this was an `Any`" when `datacontenttype` was not `application/protobuf` | **open** (questions 5 and 9) |
| extension attribute types | the arm | JSON loses them (`SWR-JSON-0023`); Protobuf does not | **open** (question 10) |
| batch | `CloudEventBatch{repeated events = 1}` | no SDK has a type for it | **open** (question 6) |

## Interoperability evidence

The offline-evidence pattern of `SWR-SEC-0008` and D-INTEROP-5: bytes recorded from the SDKs, committed with their generators and verifiers, no broker and no network in a test.
The spike committed 13 events from sdk-go v2.15.2 (protobuf-go v1.26.0) and from sdk-java 2.5.0 (protobuf-java 3.15.0), and 13 cases built by `protoc` 36.2 from the `v1.0.2` `.proto`, and one two-event batch.
Stage 1 moves what it needs from `interop/protobuf-spike/` to `interop/protobuf/` (open question 14), adds a C++ producer, and adds the Go and Java readers of the bytes the C++ side writes.
The comparison is decode-equal, never byte-equal, for the reasons in the table above; sdk-go output needs `Deterministic: true`.
A difference between the SDKs is recorded as a decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and the goldens are not edited to hide it.
Known differences already found, to be recorded as D-INTEROP-6 or the next number by the stage: `text_data` never written by sdk-go; sdk-go quotes JSON read from `text_data`; sdk-go drops a `ce_uri_ref` `dataschema`; sdk-java reads a `ce_uri_ref` extension as a URI; sdk-go percent-encodes a non-ASCII `source`.

## What this costs

- **Maintained code.** About 455 to 480 formatted lines for one event and batch, plus a test file, a fuzz target and an interop harness; CR-0006 estimated M to L and the spike measured M.
- **A hand-written parser of untrusted bytes.** It has no years of hardening behind it. The answer is the fuzz target, named limits and strict decoding, all in slice 1. The prototype's smoke run (1,000,000 mutated inputs under ASan and UBSan, no report) is not a campaign.
- **Information lost in the mapping**, listed above and stated in the guide (`SWR-PROTO-0018`).
- **Two upstream defects in sdk-go**, which this request does not fix and does not track.
- **A format that no one has asked for.** CR-0006 decision 1: no consumer waits; the bet is the owner's.

## Alternatives considered and declined

- **`libprotobuf`.** Declined by the owner on 2026-10-24: the first compiled dependency, version-matched codegen, two packaging ports, a rewording of the dependency constraint. See ADR-0015.
- **The prose numbers.** Not on the wire in either SDK.
- **A `proto_any` alternative in `data_t`.** A break; declined here and not proposed.
- **MQTT first.** The pivot named in CR-0006. The owner went ahead with Protobuf.
- **Binding integration before the format.** CR-0006 recommended format-only first; the format needs no transport.

## Stages

1. **The format, `ce::v4` addition.** Starts after the owner has answered open questions 1 to 6 and 9 to 10 that the slice needs. Spec first: the requirements of slice 1 move to `approved` as their suites are written; then tests (the `protoc` cases of the spike as input), then `protobuf_format.hpp`, `ce::format_protobuf`, the fuzz target with its seed corpus, the interop harness and goldens, the module export and `test/module_test.cpp`, the packaging recipes, the guide, the `CHANGELOG`, and `docs/DECISIONS.md` for the decisions above. The stage runs the frozen-API diff and the mutation and CRAP checks of the project.
2. **Structured mode in the bindings.** Starts after stage 1 merges and after open question 7 (names) and, if ruled in, question 6 (batch). New entry points in the HTTP, Kafka and NATS bindings under new names; the JSON entry points unchanged (`SWR-PROTO-0022`); the interop tests through the bindings.
3. **Release preparation**, following `docs/RELEASE.md`: `delivered_in` on the requirements, `docs/SPEC.md` section 1 and 4, the ten-minute fuzz on the merge commit, and the signed tag, which the owner creates. By `D-BUILD-4` a release of additions only is a patch.

## Open questions for the owner

Each has a proposal; none is decided. Questions 1 to 6, 9 and 10 gate slice 1; 7 and 8 gate slice 2.

| # | question | proposal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Decode limits** (`SWR-PROTO-0010`). The depth is fixed by the schema (event, map entry, value, `Timestamp`, `Any` opaque, groups refused), so it is a constant, not an option. What are the sizes? | Named constants in `ce::protobuf`: `default_max_encoded_size` = 16 MiB, `default_max_attribute_count` = 1024, `max_varint_bytes` = 10 (the wire format's own bound), `fixed_nesting_depth` = 4. Sizes and count are fields of `protobuf::decode_options`; both checks happen before an allocation. The values are proposals; none was measured against a workload |
| 2 | **Timestamps.** The wire carries the instant only, in years 1 to 9999; `ce::timestamp` keeps the offset and fraction digits and spans about 1678 to 2262. | Decode gives UTC with the fewest digits that hold the nanoseconds (0, 3, 6 or 9). A Timestamp outside the range is `invalid_timestamp`, as the JSON decoder refuses the same instant. Widening the type is a break and is not proposed. Stated in the guide |
| 3 | **Empty `binary_data` against no data.** sdk-go writes an empty `binary_data` for no data; sdk-java writes none. | Write no field for no data and `binary_data` of length 0 for an empty `binary`. Read an empty `binary_data` with no `datacontenttype` as no payload and with a `datacontenttype` as an empty `binary`. Alternative: always an empty `binary`, which makes every sdk-go event without data carry one |
| 4 | **JSON-typed data reads back as text or bytes.** The default decode returns `std::string` or `binary` as the wire says. | Keep the default. Add an optional `protobuf_format::decode_as<Codec>` (a new name) that classifies by `is_json_content_type`, as the bindings do, into `json_document` or `json_text` within `decode_options` retention. Also check in stage 1 that a `json_document` can be written as text without a codec argument |
| 5 | **A `proto_data` type URL that differs from `dataschema`.** | On write, use `proto_data` only for a `binary` payload with `datacontenttype` `application/protobuf` and a `dataschema`, and write `dataschema` as the type URL; otherwise write `binary_data`. On read, give the `Any` value as `binary`, set `datacontenttype` to `application/protobuf` if absent, and set `dataschema` to the type URL if absent. When both are present and differ, `dataschema` is kept and the URL is lost. A side channel without a break: `protobuf_format::decode_detailed` returning the event and the `Any` type URL, and `encode_proto_data(event, type_url)` |
| 6 | **Batch (`application/cloudevents-batch+protobuf`) in slice 1?** No SDK has a `CloudEventBatch` type; the only evidence is `protoc` bytes; the specification says the batch "MUST only be used where supported". | Yes, as format functions only (`encode_batch(std::span<const event>)`, `decode_batch(...)` returning `std::vector<event>`, about 20 lines), with the `protoc` golden as its evidence and a media type constant, and no binding batch mode. Proposed requirement: "The `protobuf_format` class shall provide `encode_batch` and `decode_batch` for a `CloudEventBatch` message, expose `application/cloudevents-batch+protobuf` as its media type, and apply the limits of `SWR-PROTO-0010` to the count of events." Alternative: not in slice 1 |
| 7 | **Binding entry-point names and how structured mode chooses its format** (slice 2). Structured mode is hard-wired to JSON. | New names per binding, for example `http::to_message_protobuf` and `http::from_message_protobuf`, and the same in Kafka and NATS, with no change to the published `from_message`. A NATS payload has no content type, so its Protobuf entry points take the format explicitly |
| 8 | **Does `from_message` detect `application/cloudevents+protobuf`?** | No. Detection would change the result of a published entry point for a message it refuses today (`SWR-PROTO-0022`) |
| 9 | **The type URL policy for `proto_data`.** | Write the URL verbatim from `dataschema`, with no `type.googleapis.com/` prefix added, as sdk-go does; accept any URL on read, since the `Any` is opaque and no registry exists in the format |
| 10 | **Extension attribute value types.** Protobuf keeps the type; JSON decode maps to `bool`, `int32` and `std::string` (`SWR-JSON-0023`). | Return the type of the wire arm, so a Protobuf round trip keeps `binary`, `uri`, `uri_ref` and `timestamp` that JSON cannot. A `ce_uri_ref` extension is read as `uri_ref`, which sdk-java does not do |
| 11 | **Additional string and byte limits** (the length of a single string or `Any`). | Covered by the encoded-size limit: no field may exceed the remaining input. No separate limit |
| 12 | **The decision date 2026-11-11 of the CR-0006 bet**, given the owner went ahead before it. | Keep it as the review of slice 1: persevere, pivot or stop, with the signals of the bet and the measured fuzz result |
| 13 | **Report the sdk-go and spec findings upstream** (`text_data` never written, the quoting, the prose against the `.proto`). | Report, if the owner agrees; this request tracks none of them |
| 14 | **Where the goldens live.** | Stage 1 moves the spike's goldens from `interop/protobuf-spike/` to `interop/protobuf/`, as `interop/kafka` is laid out, and leaves the spike's generators in place until then |
| 15 | **A plain `std::string_view` overload of `decode`.** | No; a `std::span<const std::byte>` only, and `message::body` is already that |

## Acceptance

- Slice 1: a `protobuf_format::decode` of every golden recorded from sdk-go and sdk-java returns the event they encoded, modulo the documented losses; both SDKs decode what `encode` writes to the event this SDK encoded; the decoder refuses every `protoc` edge case with the error code in the table; the fuzz target replays its corpus on every pull request and completes ten minutes in the scheduled job.
- No published declaration changes: the frozen-API diff (`python3 scripts/frozen_api_diff.py --base <last tag>`) passes, and `ce::v3`, `ce::v2` and `ce::v1` have no declaration of the format.
- The core's dependency list is unchanged: CTRE only.
- Slice 2: each binding writes and reads structured mode in Protobuf, the JSON entry points return what they return today for every input, and the interop tests pass through the bindings.
- The three specification gates and `derive_delivered_in.py --check` stay green at every commit.
